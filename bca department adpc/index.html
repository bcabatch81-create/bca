About The Department

Shaping Bright Minds in Computer Applications

The BCA Department at Anandaram Dhekial Phookan (ADP) College is committed to building robust foundations in software engineering, full-stack programming, artificial intelligence fundamentals, database management, and digital problem solving.

Our curriculum combines rigorous academic learning with hands-on lab experiments, collaborative team hackathons, and real-world industrial exposure so every student thrives with high technical competence and confidence.
